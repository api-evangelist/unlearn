# Unlearn

> Unlearn is a San Francisco AI company that builds digital twin generators (DTGs) for clinical trials — machine learning models trained on patient-level historical data that forecast an individual participant's clinical outcomes under control or standard of care. Sponsors use these AI-generated digital twins to plan trials, monitor clinical data, and run smaller, more powerful studies (reported 33% control-arm size reduction). The approach is qualified by the EMA and aligned with current FDA guidance. Unlearn is a platform company, not an API provider: it publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or machine-readable specs.
